I am a 4th-year PhD student in [**Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)**](https://ece.hku.hk/) at [**The University of Hong Kong**](https://www.hku.hk/) ([**HKU-MMLAB**](https://mmlab.hk/)), advised by [**Prof. Xihui Liu**](https://xh-liu.github.io/). My research focuses on multimodal generative AI and agent systems, especially video world models, cinematic video generation, and controllable visual content generation. Previously, I obtained my bachelor's and master's degrees at [**Shanghai Jiao Tong University**](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/) and a double master's degree (Diplome d'Ingenieur, General Engineering) at [**Ecole Centrale Paris**](https://www.centralesupelec.fr/), and conducted research internships at [**Tencent IEG ARC**](https://www.tencent.com/en-us/business.html), [**Kuaishou Kling**](https://klingai.com/), and [**Huawei Noah's Ark Lab**](https://www.noahlab.com.hk/).

My work spans three areas: (1) video world models ([Multics](https://karine-huang.github.io/Multics/) and [MemLearner](https://yujiwen.github.io/memlearner/)); (2) cinematic video and film generation ([CineScene](https://karine-huang.github.io/CineScene/) and [FilMaster](https://filmaster-ai.github.io/)); and (3) compositional image and video generation (T2I-CompBench, T2I-CompBench++, [T2V-CompBench](https://t2v-compbench-2025.github.io/), and [GenMAC](https://karine-h.github.io/GenMAC/)). The [**T2I-CompBench series**](https://github.com/Karine-Huang/T2I-CompBench) provides widely used benchmarks for compositional text-to-image generation and has been adopted in major industrial model evaluations.
